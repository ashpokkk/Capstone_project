Surcharge Payment - ATL

The late filers of Income Tax Return for Tax Year 2020 can pay “Surcharge for ATL” as defined under section 182(A) of Income Tax Ordinance 2001 by clicking on Tax Payment Nature “Misc” head in the PSID.

Only after the payment of surcharge will the name of the late filer become part of ATL.
